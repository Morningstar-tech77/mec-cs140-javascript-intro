🍗 Crispy Fried Chicken Recipe

Welcome to my fried chicken recipe! This chicken is crispy on the outside and juicy on the inside. Follow these simple steps to make it at home.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • All-purpose flour
  • Vegetable oil
  • Garlic powder
  • Paprika
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Eggs

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  2. Crack the eggs into a bowl and beat them. Dip each piece of chicken into the eggs.
  3. Put the flour in another bowl. Cover each piece of chicken with flour.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil in a deep pan. Carefully place the chicken into the hot oil.
  5. Fry the chicken until it is golden brown and fully cooked inside.
  6. Remove the chicken and place it on a paper towel to drain the extra oil. Let it rest before serving.

Enjoy Your Meal! 😋

Serve your crispy fried chicken with fries, rice, or your favorite side dish!
